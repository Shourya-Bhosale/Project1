About Shiv Organic Dairy Farm

Our Story

Shiv Organic Dairy Farm was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to bring pure, authentic A2 Gir Cow Ghee to every Indian household. Located in Bhosari, Pune, Maharashtra, we are a family-owned dairy farm committed to preserving traditional farming methods while ensuring the highest standards of quality and purity.

Our journey began with a deep respect for India's rich dairy heritage and a concern for the declining quality of dairy products in the market. We noticed that most commercial ghee was made using modern, fast methods that compromised on nutrition and authenticity. This inspired us to revive the traditional bilona method of making ghee—a process that has been used in India for thousands of years.

Today, we take pride in producing 100% pure A2 Gir Cow Ghee using the time-honored bilona churning method. Every batch is made with care, patience, and dedication to ensure you receive the most nutritious and authentic ghee possible.

Our Process

At Shiv Organic Dairy Farm, we follow the traditional bilona method to make our ghee:

  1. Fresh Milk Collection: We collect fresh milk twice daily from our Gir cows
  2. Natural Fermentation: The milk is converted to curd using natural fermentation
  3. Manual Churning: We use a wooden bilona (churner) to manually churn the curd, separating butter from buttermilk
  4. Slow Heating: The butter is heated slowly to extract pure ghee, preserving all nutrients
  5. Careful Filtering: The ghee is filtered to remove any remaining milk solids
  6. Fresh Packaging: Pure ghee is packaged in clean, airtight containers

This entire process takes 2-3 days per batch, but the result is worth it—ghee that's rich in nutrients, flavor, and authenticity.
