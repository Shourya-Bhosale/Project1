Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2024

💰 Refund Policy Overview

At Shiv Organic Dairy Farm, we strive to provide you with the best quality A2 Gir Cow Ghee. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, we offer a comprehensive refund policy to ensure your peace of mind.

✅ Refund Eligibility

  • Quality Issues: Products with visible defects, contamination, or quality problems
  • Wrong Product: If you received a different product than what you ordered
  • Damaged Delivery: Products damaged during transit or delivery
  • Unopened Products: Products returned within 7 days in original, unopened condition
  • Duplicate Orders: Accidental duplicate orders placed by mistake

❌ Non-Refundable Items

  • Opened Ghee Containers: Due to hygiene and safety reasons
  • Personal Preference: Taste preference or change of mind
  • After 7 Days: Products returned after 7 days from delivery
  • Used Products: Any product that has been partially used
  • Custom Orders: Special orders made specifically for customers

🔄 Refund Process

  1. Initiate Return: Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  2. Return Request: Call +91 9158019119 or email shivagrofoodsltd@gmail.com
  3. Approval: We'll review your request and approve if eligible
  4. Product Return: We'll arrange pickup of the product
  5. Quality Inspection: Our team will inspect the returned item
  6. Refund Processing: Refund will be processed within 3-5 business days

💳 Payment Method Refunds

UPI Payments:

  • Refunds will be processed to your registered UPI ID
  • Processing time: 1-3 business days
  • You'll receive SMS/notification from your bank

Cash on Delivery (COD):

  • Refunds will be processed via bank transfer
  • You need to provide bank account details
  • Processing time: 3-5 business days

Bank Transfer:

  • Direct transfer to your bank account
  • Processing time: 2-4 business days
  • Bank account details required

⏱️ Refund Timeline

Day 0: Return request submitted
Day 1: Request reviewed and approved
Day 2: Product pickup arranged
Day 3-4: Quality inspection completed
Day 5-7: Refund processed and credited

📋 Important Terms

  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable

  • Partial refunds may apply for partially damaged products
  • We reserve the right to refuse refunds for non-eligible items
  • Refund amount will exclude any discounts or promotional offers applied
