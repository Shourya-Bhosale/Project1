About Our Ghee

Bilona Method: We follow the ancient bilona method which is a time-honored, artisanal technique for crafting authentic ghee: fresh milk is cultured into curd, which is then hand-churned using a wooden bilona to extract butter, and finally gently heated until the water evaporates and pure, golden ghee remains

100% Natural: Our 100% Pure Ghee is crafted from thoughtfully sourced cow’s milk using traditional methods—completely free of preservatives, additives, or vegetable oils—ensuring a rich, authentic flavor, granular texture, and all the nutrition of full-fat, unadulterated ghee

Traditional Process:Our 100% Pure Gir Cow Ghee comes exclusively from A2 milk of free-grazing Gir cows, ethically raised on lush pastures under the open sky. Crafted using the revered Bilona method, this ghee preserves the rich golden hue, nutty aroma, and nutrient-packed goodness—rich in omega-3 fatty acids, vitamins A, D, E, K, and butyric acid—offering an authentic, wholesome superfood with no additives or impurities.

Grass-fed Cows: Our Ghee is made from the rich, nutritious milk of Gir cows—an indigenous breed renowned for its high-quality A2 milk. Made traditionally by slowly simmering this pure butter to retain natural flavor, aroma, and nutrients, it's free from additives, preservatives, and any artificial ingredients—just authentic, wholesome goodness in every spoonful.
