Our Commitment

At SHIV Organic Dairy Farm, we are committed to delivering the purest, most authentic A2 Gir Cow Ghee to your family. Our ghee is crafted from the milk of free-grazing Gir cows, an indigenous breed renowned for producing high-quality A2 milk. We follow strict organic farming practices, ensuring that our cows are raised ethically on lush pastures, free from hormones and antibiotics.

Our commitment extends beyond just producing ghee—we are dedicated to preserving traditional methods, supporting sustainable farming, and promoting the health benefits of pure, unadulterated dairy products. Every jar of our ghee represents our passion for quality and our respect for traditional Indian dairy practices.

Our Traditional Process

We follow the ancient Bilona method, a time-honored, artisanal technique for crafting authentic ghee. This traditional process begins with fresh milk from our Gir cows, which is cultured into curd. The curd is then hand-churned using a wooden bilona to extract butter, preserving all the natural nutrients and flavors. Finally, the butter is gently heated until the water evaporates, leaving behind pure, golden ghee with a rich, nutty aroma.

Our 100% Pure Gir Cow Ghee comes exclusively from A2 milk of free-grazing Gir cows, ethically raised on lush pastures under the open sky. This traditional process preserves the rich golden hue, nutty aroma, and nutrient-packed goodness—rich in omega-3 fatty acids, vitamins A, D, E, K, and butyric acid—offering an authentic, wholesome superfood with no additives or impurities.
