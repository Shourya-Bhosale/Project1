# WhatsApp Integration Options - Do You Need Facebook?

**For WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta)**: Yes, you need a **Meta Business Account** (separate from personal Facebook)

**But**: You DON'T need:
- ❌ Personal Facebook account
- ❌ Facebook Page
- ❌ Facebook Business Page
- ❌ Instagram account

You ONLY need a **Meta Business Account** (it's just for accessing Meta's services)

## Setup Steps (Minimal Facebook Interaction)

1. Go to **https://business.facebook.com/**
2. Create Meta Business Account (use business email, not personal)
3. Verify business (email verification)
4. Connect WhatsApp Business number
5. Get API credentials
6. Done! ✅

**Total Facebook/Page involvement: ZERO**
**You're just using their business infrastructure**
