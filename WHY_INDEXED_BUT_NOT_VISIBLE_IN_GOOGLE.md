## Why This Happens

### 1. **Low Search Ranking** (Most Common)
**Your page is indexed, but it's not ranking high enough to appear on first page.**

**Why:**
- New website (low domain authority)
- Not enough backlinks
- Not enough search traffic history
- Competing with established sites

**What Google shows:**
- Only top 10 results on first page
- Your site might be on page 2, 3, or 10+
- You need to scroll through many pages to find it

---

### 2. **Search Query Mismatch**
**You're searching for terms that don't match your page content.**

**Try searching for:**
- `site:shivorganicdairyfarms.com` (to see all indexed pages)
- `"Shiv Organic Dairy Farm"` (exact match)
- `"A2 Gir Cow Ghee Pune"` (specific keywords)
- `"Bhosari ghee"` (location-based)

**Don't search for:**
- Generic terms like "ghee" (too competitive)
- Single words like "dairy" (too broad)

---

### 3. **Google Search Results Are Cached**
**Google updates search results gradually, not instantly.**

**Timeline:**
- **Indexed:** ✅ Already done
- **Appearing in results:** Takes 1-4 weeks
- **Ranking improvement:** Takes 3-6 months

**Even though it's indexed, it may take time to appear in search results.**

---

### 4. **Geographic/Local Search Issues**
**Your site might not appear for local searches yet.**

**If you're searching from:**
- Different location than Pune
- Different country
- Google might prioritize local results

**Try:**
- Search with location: "A2 ghee Pune"
- Use Google Maps search
- Search from Pune location

---

### 5. **Personalized Search Results**
**Google shows different results to different people.**

**Factors:**
- Your search history
- Your location
- Your device
- Your Google account

**What you see ≠ What others see**
